# Topia – Global Mobility Platform

> Topia helps enterprises manage global talent mobility, tax compliance, and distributed workforce operations in one platform. Founded in 2010, Topia serves HR, Finance, and Legal teams at mid-market and enterprise companies operating across borders.

## Products

- [Global Talent Mobility](https://topia.com/products/global-talent-mobility): End-to-end management of international assignments, relocations, and mobile workforce compliance.
- [Tax & Compliance Engine](https://topia.com/products/tax-and-compliance-engine): Automated tax withholding, Permanent Establishment risk detection, and audit-ready records.
- [Cost Estimation & Tracking](https://topia.com/products/cost-estimation-and-tracking): Real-time cost forecasting and budget management for global moves and assignments.
- [Global Workforce Planning](https://topia.com/products/global-workforce-planning): Strategic planning and optimization of distributed talent distribution.
- [Cost & Immigration Risk Management](https://topia.com/products/cost-and-immigration-risk-management): Proactive immigration compliance and risk mitigation across jurisdictions.
- [Remote Work & Compliance](https://topia.com/products/remote-work-and-compliance): Policy management and compliance monitoring for remote and hybrid workers.
- [Business Travel](https://topia.com/products/business-travel): Pre-travel assessments, day-count tracking, and business travel compliance automation.
- [Topia Horizon](https://topia.com/horizon): AI-powered agentic workspace for global mobility teams (early access).
